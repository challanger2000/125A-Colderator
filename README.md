# 125A Colderator

**Analog warmth is over — welcome to digital cold.**

125A Colderator is a creative VST3 audio effect concept focused on translating the physical and visual associations of cold into sound: frost, ice, snow, frozen metal, shivering, distance and winter-like sterility.

## Core product idea

The plugin is built around one primary macro control:

- **COLD** — overall intensity of the cold transformation

Supporting controls are intentionally limited and must justify their existence by adding a distinct perceptual dimension.

Current research directions include:
- ICE — crystalline / glass-like structure
- METAL — inharmonic metallic character
- FROST — fine high-frequency roughness / frozen surface texture
- SHIVER — controlled micro-modulation
- SPACE — sparse, cold spatial character
- OUTPUT — level trim

## Design rules

- 0% must be neutral where specified.
- 50% must sound meaningfully like 50%, not merely a subtle hint.
- 100% must represent the intended maximum.
- Musical pitch and chord identity should remain intact through the normal working range.
- Extreme 90–100% settings may intentionally become strongly resonant, metallic or effect-like.
- The design must not collapse into a simple EQ, exciter, bitcrusher, ring modulator or fixed resonator.
- DSP decisions must be derived, measured and verified according to the current **125A-Engineering** standards.
- External projects may be used as research references only within their license boundaries.
